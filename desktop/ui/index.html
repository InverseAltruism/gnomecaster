Step 1 of 3

Find World of Warcraft

Gnomecaster adds a small addon to your game folder. Choose the game version you play.

World of Warcraft wasn't found automatically

Choose its folder: the one that contains _retail_, _classic_ or _classic_era_, usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft.

Install the addon

The addon is the panel you talk to in the game. It goes into the game's AddOns folder:

Interface\AddOns

Connect an AI

OpenRouter gives one account access to many AI models. You pay OpenRouter directly; a typical question costs under a tenth of a cent.

OpenRouter opened in your browser. Approve Gnomecaster there and this window continues by itself.

Choose a model

You're set

  1. 1

    Start World of Warcraft. If it was running, restart it so the addon loads.

  2. 2

    Type /gc in chat and ask anything. Prefer a hotkey? Set one in Options › Keybindings › AddOns › Gnomecaster.

Keep this app running while you play. Closing its window keeps it running: its icon is under the arrow next to the clock.

    Gnomecaster couldn't start

    Quit Gnomecaster from its tray icon and open it again. If this keeps happening, the log folder in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Gnomecaster\logs says why.
